## 2. MAIA COST Action (CA23141, 2024–2028)

### 2.1 Mission & Scale
* **Full Title:** *Managing Artificial Intelligence in Archaeology* (MAIA).
* **Funding Framework:** Horizon Europe COST (European Cooperation in Science and Technology).
* **Leadership:** Action Chair: Prof. Gabriele Gattiglia (University of Pisa); Vice-Chair: Dr. Holly Wright (University of York).
* **Consortium:** 30+ European member states, 150+ researchers.

### 2.2 Working Group Architecture & ArchaeoPhD AI Alignment
* **WG1: State of the Art:** Synthesizing current applications of AI/ML in archaeology.
* **WG2: Training Collections & Machine-Actionable Data:** Enforcing FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) on archaeological training datasets. ArchaeoPhD AI contributes by exporting standardized JSON-LD graphs.
* **WG3: Ethics, Bias, Epistemology & Transparency:** Formulating ethical guidelines to combat algorithmic opacity and black-box hallucinations. ArchaeoPhD AI aligns by replacing "100% fidelity" marketing with reproducible open benchmarks ($R_{\text{cand}}$, $F_{1,\text{bind}}$, FAR) and mandatory human-in-the-loop review.
* **WG4: Communication & Policy Recommendations:** Translating AI ethics into policy for national heritage bodies.

## 3. Horizon Europe AUTOMATA (GA 101158046, €4.88M, 2024–2029)

### 3.1 Mission & Scale
* **Full Title:** *AUTOMated enriched digitisation of Archaeological liThics and cerAmics* (AUTOMATA).
* **Funding:** Horizon Europe Research and Innovation Programme (€4.88M).
* **Coordinator:** Prof. Gabriele Gattiglia (University of Pisa); 12 international partner institutions.
* **Core Technology:** AI-augmented robotics (cobots) executing high-throughput 3D photogrammetry, microscopic texture analysis, and non-invasive archaeometry on stone tools (lithics) and pottery fragments (ceramics).

### 3.2 Complementary Role of ArchaeoPhD AI
* AUTOMATA captures the physical digital twin of the artifact and ingests it into the **European Collaborative Cloud for Cultural Heritage (ECCCH)**.
* ArchaeoPhD AI acts as AUTOMATA's literature counterpart: extracting the historical excavation records, locus sheets, and compliance reports that document the original physical provenance of those exact artifacts, linking them via the **ECCCH Heritage Digital Twin Ontology (HDTO)**.

## 4. ERC AviArch (GA 101125532, €2.0M, 2024–2029) & ArchAIDE

### 4.1 Mission & Scale
* **Full Title:** *AVIfauna in ARCHaeoecological networks* (AviArch).
* **Funding:** European Research Council (ERC) Consolidator Grant (€2.0M).
* **Leadership:** Principal Investigator: Prof. Beatrice Demarchi (University of Turin); Co-Investigator: Prof. Gabriele Gattiglia (AI and digital frameworks).

### 4.2 Empirical Accuracy Benchmarks (Dubbini et al. 2026)
In their seminal peer-reviewed study (*"Multimodal fusion of visual and morphometric features for avian bone classification"*, July 2026, *arXiv:2607.26743*), Dubbini, Yeomans, Pavia, Parmaksiz, Hooglugt, Gattiglia, and Demarchi reported real-world multimodal performance across $>10,000$ specimens:
* **Skeletal Element Identification (Bone Type):** **86% test accuracy**.
* **Family-Level Taxonomic Classification:** **51% Top-1 accuracy**, rising to **75% Top-3 accuracy**.

### 4.3 Significance for ArchaeoPhD AI v2.0
* Domain specialists in archaeological AI do not expect or trust claims of "100% flawless autonomous extraction."
* ArchaeoPhD AI adopts the **Top-k Candidate Paradigm**: presenting the human specialist with the Top-3 highest-confidence entity bindings, cutting human verification time by $\sim 70\%$ while ensuring 100% human accountability.
